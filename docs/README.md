# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to OctoAcme’s project management guidance. These documents describe how cross-functional projects are initiated, planned, delivered, released, and improved.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative approach with clear ownership and measurable outcomes. Work begins by validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and agreeing on success criteria. The Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders then follow a structured lifecycle: initiate and authorize the work, plan the prioritized backlog and milestones, track execution through the project board, manage risks and dependencies, and verify quality through testing and review.

Delivery is organized around regular standups, PM–PdM alignment, milestone reviews, and stakeholder updates. Blockers are triaged by the team and escalated through the PM and Product Lead to dependent teams or sponsors when needed. Quality practices include acceptance criteria, small pull requests, automated CI tests and linting, required approvals, unit and integration testing, critical-flow smoke tests, and security scans. Releases use readiness checks, deployment verification, rollback planning, incident response, and stakeholder announcements.

At the end of a sprint, release, incident, or milestone, the team holds a retrospective to capture what went well, identify improvements, and assign owned action items with due dates. This feedback loop turns lessons into tracked backlog work and supports consistent, repeatable delivery.
